# GundariuM Brand Guidelines
*For collaborators and social media use*

---

## Wordmark

**GUNDARIU<M>**

The GundariuM wordmark is set in **Orbitron** (weight: Black / 900), all caps, with wide tracking.

- `GUNDARIU` — rendered in **Cyan** `#00d4ff`
- `M` — rendered in **Purple** `#7c3aed`

The two-tone split on the trailing **M** is the defining mark of the brand. It must always appear this way — never all one color, never swapped.

The wordmark may appear on dark backgrounds only. Do not place it on white or light surfaces.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Headings, wordmark, buttons, labels | **Orbitron** | Black (900) / Bold (700) |
| Body copy, descriptions | **Geist Sans** | Regular |
| Code / addresses | **Geist Mono** | Regular |

Orbitron is a Google Font available at no cost. Use it for any GundariuM-branded graphics.

---

## Tone

- Mechanical and precise — not casual or playful
- Combat-ready — this is a battle game
- No emoji in branded copy
- Reference the Gundam universe with respect — players know it well

## What Not to Do

- Do not alter the cyan/purple split on the wordmark
- Do not use the wordmark on light backgrounds
- Do not use rarity colors outside of card/trait contexts
- Do not represent GundariuM as affiliated with Bandai Namco or Sunrise — it is fan-inspired, not licensed
- Do not promise specific token values or investment returns in social copy
